Puqian, a fishing town in Wenchang, Hainan.

Centuries ago, nearby villages sank beneath the sea. Today the town is slipping away more quietly — while the world rushes forward, a few still mend their nets, sort the day's catch, and keep the old rhythms. I photographed what remains, before the tide takes it too.
